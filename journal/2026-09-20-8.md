# 2026-09-20 — Session 8: orders by courier, and how the Navy did it

Transcript: `sessions/2026-09-20-8.transcript.md` (same conversation as sessions 5–7).

Branch `worktree-phase1a`, PR #42.

## What was done

- Showed the revised 1a preview (seed 4242): ~one letter a week, the orders dialog, rumours beneath reports, an off-lane scout bringing home a governor's stranded letters.
- Three more user changes: the dialog says *last known location: World, N wk ago*; any hull leaving a port takes a **copy** of every report waiting there (original stays for the packet; first copy read wins, the rest are discarded); every *report received* in a dossier links to its letter.
- Researched how orders reached ships in the age of sail — Post Office packets on scheduled routes alongside the Navy's advice boats; orders addressed to stations, ports and rendezvous, held by the port for the ship; mission-style instructions with reporting from friendly ports (sources in the transcript and decisions.md). Concluded the setting maps onto it directly.
- Built the result: `sendByCourier()` routes a letter along a hull's run and dispatches it there and back; the dialog offers *last known / its destination / its rendezvous* as addresses and a "dispatch ▾hull to carry these orders" line where no packet goes or a hull would land sooner; `hexRoute()` moved to `chart.ts` as public knowledge. The user's rule: a hull takes stranded mail (and copies) only if its run reaches a port on lanes connected to the destination. Verified in the browser: orders for a patrol craft holding at off-lane Grikzeth went by courier "via Sekthetzik → Mouskail → Grikzeth", were read, and both hulls came home.
- Filed `#43` (captured packets are captured dispatches; packets sink the mail) and `#44` (call at the rendezvous for orders) for 1b.

## Decisions (rationale in decisions.md)

- "The courier is the mail" over nested orders.
- Stranded mail loaded only where the hull's run reaches its destination.
