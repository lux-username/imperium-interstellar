## What was done

Six commits, one per issue group, each with tests, in the order STATUS had planned:

- **Events and channels** (`#22`, `#23`). `events.ts` records unrest steps, governor changes, hull arrivals/departures and letters received; `newGame()` clears anything that predates the desk. `Report.channel`, `Report.events`, an `event` snapshot kind, `PlayerView.rumours` split from `inbox`. Save format v2.
- **Traits, hulls, orders, movement** (`#11` part, `#18`, `#19`). `characters.ts` rolls loyalty/initiative/competence/ambition. `ShipRole` widened; `Ship.strength` and `standing.rally`. `orders.ts` carries progress on the order — courier `leg` (fixes the `indexOf` ambiguity), patrol `began`, scout `lookedOn`. `ships.ts` took movement out of `mail.ts`: charted route first, hex route within jump range for non-packets; commander reports at the ordered world and at patrol end; a scout looks for a week. Sent hulls take stranded off-lane mail, which is re-routed at the next charted port; ports keep only the newest six uncollected reports.
- **Starting fleet and orders from the desk** (`#24`). `fleet.ts`: 4 patrol, 2 escort, 2 transport, 4 couriers, 2 scouts with rolled commanders. `orderShip()` addresses the dispatch to the last sighting. `PlayerView.roster` (identity only). UI: Fleet tab, order form in every dossier, orders in Outgoing, event lists in letters.
- **Governors write on events** (`#20`). `governors.ts`: disclosure roll 2d6 ≥ 8 with severity, −3 for self-loyal on implicating news, −2 bold / +1 cautious on news that asks for help; good news always; new governors introduce themselves; routine traffic never; "all quiet" every 8–12 weeks by hex; self-serving governors shade unrest by two. A letter from the desk is now a `dispatch_received` event the governor answers with everything since their last letter they will admit to. `REPORT_INTERVAL` removed.
- **Rumours** (`#25`, `#26`). `rumours.ts`: events at ports become talk (bad 2d6 ≥ 7, good ≥ 9), hop one lane a week at 2d6 ≥ 9, degrade (week ±2 sometimes, neighbouring world rarely, never the capital), and are delivered as `merchant` (≤ 1 hop) or `docks` reports to anyone with a belief state at that world. UI: the Rumours tab, per-world "what the docks say".
- Browser check with Playwright: ordered a scout from a dossier, advanced six weeks, saw the commander's letter arrive and the hull back on the Fleet tab. Three cosmetic fixes came out of it (short event labels for subjects, rumours never about the capital, "uneasy" for unrest 1).
- Spec touched for what shipped: neutral valence, commander letters, off-lane jumps for sent hulls, hulls carrying stranded mail.

## Decisions (rationale in decisions.md)

- A letter from the desk is an event the governor answers, not a direct call.
- Sent hulls carry stranded mail and re-route it at the next charted port.
- Commanders write at the ordered world and at patrol end, not at every port.
