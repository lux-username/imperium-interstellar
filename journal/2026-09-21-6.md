# 2026-09-21 — Session 12: names merged, playtest begun, letters given subjects and ledes

Opened with the user asking where the project stood against the phases: Phase 0 and 1a closed, 1b built and waiting on its playtest, 1c filed and unstarted. The user had wired the name pools in another session (branch `worktree-names-wiring`, draft PR #56); on their word I marked it ready and merged it into `main` at `c91f682`, then relaunched the prototype from `main` in the browser.

The user played, and came back with the first finding of the 1b playtest (`#34`): the game functions, but the information it gives — not too little, that is the design — is insufficiently conveyed. Three asks, with examples: letters should have a subject line and a first sentence carrying the most important news (*Hawera governor's report: all quiet / The world is quiet.*; *Ajax captain report: defeated by pirates / The pirates outnumbered us and I called a retreat.*); the "from" should carry a title and a captain's ship (*Governor Sarah Bradley*, *Captain Amelia Middleton — Blackwing*); and captains should send quick one-event updates after a battle, a sighting or another event, and a general report of everything since their last orders on reaching the rendezvous — depending on personality, and only from a world on the courier network. Standard phrasing for now; personality variants later.

Built on `worktree-letters-subjects` (draft PR #58). New `src/sim/letters.ts` composes a `Heading` (subject, lede) for every report from the writer's side and an `Occasion` — a governor's letter or a requested one, a captain's arrival, look, patrol's end, quick update, general report, a scout's watch, prisoners' notes — leading with the worst event by severity (battles outrank the hulls lost in them) and counting the rest as *(+n)*. Sides matter: a landing is *Reinforcements landed* to the side it favours and *Enemy landing!* to the other; a battle's outcome is read off the hulls the letter says were lost or broke off, so a self-serving captain who leaves out her own retreat also gets a rosier subject. Hostile hulls lying in port make an arrival letter a sighting. The event wording (`eventLabel`, `eventText`, the unrest words) moved from `src/ui/format.ts` into the sim and is re-exported through `view.ts`, so the inbox says only what a letter said. `Report` gained `observerTitle`, `observerShip`, `subject`, `lede`; `writeReport` fills them from the writer's post.

Captains: `commanderReport` takes the occasion. `afterActionReports` now considers anything serious, hostile arrivals and whatever befell the ship (own damage was missing from the letter; added), writes a fight of the ship's own unconditionally (carried aboard where there is no port to post from) and anything else only from a `friendlyPort` and on a 2d6 check vs 7, cautious +3, bold −3. `Ship.lastOrders` and `Ship.log` were added; `logWitnessed` runs before the week's sailings and copies each week's events at the ship's world into her log (a hull that fled logs the fight she left); reading an order stamps `lastOrders` and clears the log; `runEndsAt` decides that an arrival is the rendezvous (the order is done here and what follows — `then` or the rally point — is here or nowhere), and `onArrival` then writes the general report from the log plus the week, headed *General report: <worst of it>* with *We have made the rendezvous at X; the most serious news since your orders of wk N is that …*. Not written at the capital, where the desk sees the hull directly. Save format bumped to 6.

UI: inbox rows head `<World> governor's report: <Subject>` / `<Ship> captain's report: <Subject>` / `<Ship> watch report: …`; the from-line is the signature; the opened letter starts with the lede in bold, then the standard particulars, then events prefixed with world and week where they differ from the letter's. The dossier signs its report lines the same way.

Verified in the browser on `main`'s seed: governors' rows read *Cévennes governor's report: Rhaetia robbed (+4)*, *Pirates sighted!*, *Unrest rising*, *All quiet*; sent *Champion* to patrol Cévennes and read *Champion captain's report: Arrived at Cévennes* and *… Patrol of Cévennes complete* signed *Captain Cao Jinrong — Champion*. 339 tests (12 new in `letters.test.ts`), typecheck and lint clean.

Two judgment calls, stated on `#59` for a ruling: routine arrival letters at every friendly lane port are kept alongside the general report; a fight off the network is still written and carried rather than waiting for the rendezvous.

Decisions: letter headings composed in the sim, not the desk; captains' occasions and the general report at the rendezvous (see `decisions.md`).
