## What was done

Showed the user the 1a build in a browser at week 32 of a game with a patrol and two scouts out: inbox leading with events, the Rumours and Fleet tabs, the dossier with its order form. The user liked it and asked for four changes, all made:

1. **Unrest thresholds.** Events only when a world changes mood (content 0–1, neutral 2–5, hostile 6–10) or reaches 0 or 10; an extreme is reported once per visit, checked against the last unrest event in the log rather than new state. Rumours follow automatically since they spawn from events. Measured on a fresh seed: 21 letters and 4 rumours in 20 weeks, from ~2.5 letters a week before.
2. **One orders dialog.** `OrdersDialog.tsx` replaces the inline form: hull, where to send the order (default last sighting, editable, with the week it should land), destination, task (hold / patrol N wk / look for a week), disposition, afterwards (proceed to a world or hold). Opened from "Send a hull here…" on a world or "Give orders…" on a Fleet row. `orderShip()` takes the address and standing orders; `Ship.standing.onContact` added.
3. **Commanders write from every friendly port on the lanes**, by the next packet; off the lanes only at the ordered world, carried. Supersedes the session-6 destination-only rule.
4. **Rumours off the map.** `learn()` ignores event snapshots; the dossier lists reports above what the docks say.

Verified the dialog end to end in the browser (a courier sent to look at a far world with "never engage", followed by its own en-route letter). One self-inflicted bug caught there: a CSS edit had cut the inbox row rules; restored.

## Decisions (rationale in decisions.md)

- Unrest thresholds, extremes once per visit.
- Commander letters from every friendly port (supersedes same-day destination-only rule).
- Rumour kept out of belief entirely.
- One orders dialog over per-world forms.
