## What was done

Continued straight on from session 9 with the user present. Each ruling was built and tested as it came:

- **No faster-than-light closures.** A packet lane had been closing when the *desk* believed the far port hostile — a leak. Now a port holds its packet back only when the rumour of the fall has reached that port's own docks (or, leaving a seat, the seat's own mail). The Warlord's targets likewise come only from his own belief.
- **Port guns as a bonus, not a wall.** A 3 / B 2 / C 1, counted for whoever is docked; docked hulls stand; hunters weigh the approaches first and the port separately. Battles per 80 weeks roughly doubled.
- **The Warlord's mind.** Rebuilt on five rulings: reinforce his own worlds, land where it hurts the desk most and he believes he can win (warships his people saw, port guns if docked), take independents, hunt cheap prizes, scout silent governors first; treason removed until money. Then: pirates as his enemies too (nests at his own havens first; pirates get no port guns at a haven); an **officer pool** of his own that crews prizes and replaces governors who breed pirates; and finally missions **planned against the tank and staged from forward bases**, rallying at the base nearest the target — after the user caught my claim that "two out, two back fits his tanks", which the code had never enforced.
- **Events know their sides.** `against`/`favours` on every event; governors and the docks read news from their own faction. Fixed the Warlord's governors confessing his victories as disasters.
- **Fuel by jumps**, refuel at B+, repair at C+, last-jump diversion to fuel (rendezvous first), propagated through pirate and Warlord decisions. **Couriers folded into scouts**; packets kept separate as timetable infrastructure. **Warships carry a detachment.** **Captured pirates are questioned** about havens, with the odd false name.
- Bugs found along the way: a captured hull "fleeing" as a prize (the side filter compared it with itself); the Warlord being council-replaced at his own seat; `Infinity` fuel not surviving JSON; a landing wiped out on the way in leaving no event.

Filed `#55` and marked economy/money and troop recruitment in the spec as needing a later pass.
