The user answered every point in one message, and this session recorded the answers:

- **Milestones.** 1a *Hulls and people*, 1b *Trouble*, 1c *The Empire*, each ending in a playtest issue as `#16` did. Existing issues assigned; 19 new ones filed (`#22`–`#40`); `#13` closed with its answers folded into spec.md.
- **Events and channels.** Events are the unit of news; `Report.channel` replaces the fidelity idea; rumours get a separate inbox section ("what you hear").
- **Standing.** The user's steer: bad rumours must not move standing themselves, only run the liar check; the monthly report carries ± per topic; being caught lying must be worse than honestly reporting failure. Spec now has a placeholder table (start 12, ±1 per topic, tribute ±1/−2, merchants −1, caught −5 with a three-visit memory, liar check 2d6 ≥ 8 / ≥ 6) and the invariants tuning must keep.
- **Scale and aid.** Starting scale doubled (8 warships, 4 couriers, 2 scouts, 4 army + 2 marines, 12 months upkeep; Warlord 6/6; 4–6 pirates). Aid doubled, one request per disaster topic, extra tribute buys an extra unit. Parade = Warlord captured and Council pleased at term end.
- **Map.** Colour is allegiance; age is the coloured badge and nothing else — not opacity.
- **Warlord.** Tracked belief state like the player's; sends scouts.
- **Agents and scouts.** Agents watch a world for N weeks and write the one fully accurate report (state, every hull's arrival/departure/refuelling, enemy scouts seen), then come home; they do not touch rumour. Scout ships: tiny two-person J-2 hulls with a large escape bonus, for every side. Agent scouting moved from Phase 2 into 1b; subversion and protection stay Phase 2.

Design consequence noted for `#11`: `Event`, `channel`, `'scout'` role, an agent post, a per-world traffic log, and a `beliefs[warlord]` entry.

## Decisions (rationale in decisions.md)

- Three Phase 1 milestones over one.
- Events as the unit of news.
- `Report.channel` over `Report.fidelity`.
- Standing moved by the player's words, tribute, and merchants — never by rumour directly.
- The Warlord as a believer with scouts.
- Agents as accurate observers, not rumour verifiers.
- The imperial capital as an off-map node on a border lane.
- Colour for allegiance, the badge for age.
