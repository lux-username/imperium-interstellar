# 2026-09-19 — session 1: design and scaffold

Transcript: `sessions/2026-09-19-1.transcript.md`

## What was done

- Read two *Tales to Astound!* "Out of the Box" essays (the hard-SF one from 2015 and the distant-isolated-worlds one from 2016) and extracted the two principles this game is built on: pulp planetary-romance tone rather than hard SF, and isolation produced by mechanics (low jump numbers, TL caps, charted lanes vs. dark worlds, fuel risk) as experienced from the ship, not the map.
- Discussed and settled the legal footing: implement mechanics in our own words, own setting, no trademarks or third-party names. PDFs moved to gitignored `references/`.
- Laid out the game vision in conversation and wrote it up as `spec.md`: seven pillars, information model (ground truth vs. reports; location-addressed delivery; held dispatches), order vocabulary with engagement postures, characters as the gameplay, the Empire as delegation upward, four phases.
- Resolved the player-mobility question: person with a location, fixed at the capital for Phase 1, capital modelled as the delivery address with an `acting_governor` slot so Phase 2 mobility (deputy holds the seal) is an addition, not a rewrite.
- Scaffolded the project: workflow skeleton (README, CLAUDE.md, spec, STATUS, decisions, MAP, journal/, sessions/), per-repo skills, Vite 8 + React 19 + TypeScript + Vitest 5, `src/sim/rng.ts` (seeded PRNG, 2d6 helpers) and `src/sim/hex.ts` (odd-q grid, parsec distance) with 12 passing tests, empty Inbox | Map UI shell.
- Created public repo `lux-username/imperium-interstellar`, pushed the initial commit, created milestone *Phase 0 — Belief map* with issues #1–#9.

## Decisions (rationale in decisions.md)

- Clean-room mechanics over licensed/copied text.
- TypeScript web app over Python TUI or Godot.
- One-week turns over real-time-with-pause.
- Location-addressed information model; player fixed at the capital in Phase 1.
- Subsector scale; governor-general as default player role.
- Vitest 5 over Vitest 3 (Vite 8 type compatibility).
