# 2026-09-21 — Session 13: playtest rounds two and three, on one long branch

Opened with a page of playtest notes from the user, under three heads: pirates, a map rework, and minor changes. Everything went on `worktree-playtest-2` (PR #60, sixteen commits by the end), and the user kept playing on the branch from a dev server on port 5199 while ruling on each question as it came up, so the second half of the session was a conversation with code between the turns.

**The notes, as delivered.** Pirates: corrupt governors say nothing of their own pirates (not in port, not coming, not going; a battle they may still report), a captain who sees a pirate docked unseized writes home about the governor and the world is marked a known haven until the governor changes; pirates rob nothing at a haven they know; fewer of them (3, spawn 0.005). Map: hulls drawn per ship in faction colours, clickable, with a dossier on the right — captain, last sighting, orders (ours only), reports *from* the hull or *all mentioning* her — the chosen hull ringed and its orders drawn along the lanes; a week scrubber that turns the map back and shows what Government House *now* knows of that week, which needed sightings to carry the week an event happened; a past/present toggle on the dossiers. Minor: no "Settled by"; scouts have fuel scoops; an Officers tab; rumours never arrive the week of the event; "send the full truth" reworded; an Enemy hulls tab; rumour chance halved (it was 2d6 ≥ 7 bad / ≥ 9 good).

**Belief as a fold.** The scrubber forced the shape: `src/sim/belief.ts` holds `absorb`/`beliefFrom`, shared by `learn()` and the UI, and an event in a letter that names a hull is a sighting of her at that world on that week — a general report from week 20 saying a raider made port at X in week 14 places her at X as of 14. Government House's own observations and the inherited survey became delivered mail (`view.observations`), so the whole pile re-folds as of any week; a test asserts the re-fold equals what the sim learned. `Report.observerShipId`, `RosterEntry.commander`, `view.havens`, `view.names`; save format 7.

**Then the rulings, in order.** Rumours have no good/bad preference (one target). C ports fuel — the user asked why not, and on reviewing every port rule (a table of A–X against lanes, fuel, repair, guns, havens, rumours, the Warlord's bases) the B-or-better fuel rule was the odd one out and was what stranded pirates at C havens. Pirates raid any world on the lanes, not only A/B. Dockyards: a knocked-out hull (damage ≥ strength — a hulk, every prize) is rebuilt only at B or better; C patches a hull that still fights; sightings carry `hulk`; a knocked-out pirate at a C haven limps to one with a yard. Pirates talk: hulls lying together pool their havens; a hull docked at a haven hears the docks — harboured or touted talk adds a haven, a seizure strikes one off; a bold self-serving governor touts the port (2d6 ≥ 10 a week), talk from the start, never an event. Talk travels by severity (3 → 2d6 ≥ 7, 2 → ≥ 9, 1 → ≥ 11). Rumours mark no haven, ever — pinned by tests. Governors no longer shade unrest by two: their lie is what they leave out. Packets never fight: the battle event now names the intruders and records whether the port's side had guns of its own, and a governor with only the batteries says the port fought. Port guns halved (A 2, B 1, nothing below): a lone raider now robs at a B port. "The desk" became "the Home Office" and, on reflection that *home* is the metropole, **Government House** — spec, comments, letters, panes; the id prefix went `r-desk-` → `r-home-` → `r-gh-` with the old ones still read. Worldgen: 2d6 ≥ 9 per hex (~22 worlds), C spurs at two parsecs and lone C ports bridged, and `generateSubsector` rerolls until the layout has a chokepoint, a far corner, a chart the capital reaches and a few dark worlds (three in four raw layouts pass; seeds 1 and 2 eyeballed and liked). Map: the ring sits on the triangle clicked (the row no longer re-sorts on selection). "Hold there" is "go there", with a hint that *Afterwards* decides whether she stays. Scouts: unnamed crews (no officer rolled, letters signed *Scout Wren*, `crewed()`/`wantsOfficer()` replace the commander checks), the watch is theirs alone and patrol is greyed out for them, they never join an action — in the open they run, at the quay they sit it out, and pirates ignore them.
